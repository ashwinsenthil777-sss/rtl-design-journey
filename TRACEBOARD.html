Learn by building

Wire a real loop, then test it

Drag parts onto the board, wire them into a loop, then hit Simulate. This is a real, working logic check — not a mockup.

Add a battery, a switch, and an LED — then wire them into one continuous loop.

Every part uses a real schematic symbol, just like in KiCad's Eeschema. Parts snap to a grid — drag to move · click a part then press R to rotate or Del to remove it. Press W to enter Wire Mode, then click one pin then another — wires auto-route at right angles, just like KiCad's orthogonal wire tool. Hover any pin to see its whole connected net light up. Red-pulsing pins are unconnected. Click a switch or push button to flip it, or a wire to delete it. Esc cancels an in-progress wire. Open ⚙ Preferences to set grid, units, wire routing, and board theme, just like KiCad's Preferences dialog.
